## Android dependency resolution

The uploaded Debug and Release logs both fail while resolving Material 3
artifacts at version `1.5.0`. The build log reports that these coordinates are
not found in Google Maven or Maven Central. The app now pins the Material 3
artifacts to `1.5.0-rc01`, the latest 1.5.0 line that was published before the
CI run, and forces that version to avoid the Compose BOM selecting the
unavailable `1.5.0` coordinate.
